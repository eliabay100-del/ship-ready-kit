Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 January 2026

Who we are

This is a fixture site used to test launch-readiness tooling. It is not a real product and it does not sell anything. The controller for any personal data described here is the fixture maintainer, reachable at hello@example.com. Because this site exists to demonstrate good practice, it deliberately collects as little as possible and explains every field it does collect.

What we collect

If you accept analytics cookies, we record a random session identifier, the pages you visit, the referring page, and coarse device information such as browser family and screen size. We do not record your name, email address, precise location, keystrokes, or the contents of any form, because the fixture has no forms. If you reject cookies, no analytics script is loaded and no identifier is created. Server logs record the request path, response status, and a truncated IP address for security and debugging, and those logs are deleted after thirty days.

Why we process it

Analytics data is used only to understand which pages are visited and whether the site is fast enough. Security logs are used to detect abuse and to diagnose errors. We rely on your consent for analytics and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service available and secure for the logs. We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Cookies and consent

The consent banner appears on your first visit. Accepting loads the analytics script and stores your choice in local storage under the key cookie-consent. Rejecting stores the same key with a rejected value and loads nothing. You can change your mind at any time by clearing that key, which makes the banner appear again. No non-essential cookie is set before you choose, and refusing is exactly as easy as accepting.

How long we keep it

Analytics events are aggregated after fourteen months and the raw records are deleted. Security logs are deleted after thirty days. Support email is deleted after twenty-four months unless it is needed to resolve an open dispute. Backup copies expire on a rolling thirty-five day cycle, so a deletion request is fully reflected everywhere within at most thirty-five days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email hello@example.com with the subject line Data Request and we will respond within thirty days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will never charge you for exercising these rights, and we will not degrade the service if you do.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects your rights, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again. The previous version remains available on request. Questions about this policy can be sent to the address above.
