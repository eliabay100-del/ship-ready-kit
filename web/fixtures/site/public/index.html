Frequently asked questions

What does this fixture prove?

That the crawler, SEO, accessibility, mobile, consent, and performance specs can all go green on a correct site.

Why not test against production?

Because load, chaos, and consent tests create traffic and state. Staging is for breaking things.

Does it cover the backend?

No. The distributed kit handles invariants, queues, and failure modes.

How are accessibility issues found?

Automated axe rules cover roughly a third of WCAG. Keyboard and screen-reader passes cover the rest.

What happens on a failed release?

Roll back first, then diagnose. The kill criteria are agreed before launch, not during an incident.
